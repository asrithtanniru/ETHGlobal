# Reach

**Who it's for.** People working abroad who pay family bills back home, and the billers they pay, such as schools, clinics and landlords.

**Can they try it today.** `/app/bills` reads any payee's inbox with no wallet. Posting a bill currently uses the `post-bill` command, not a web form.

**After Mumbai.** A biller web form is the next step, so a school can post without a terminal.
